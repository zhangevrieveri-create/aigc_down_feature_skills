# Style profile: Nature-family papers (nature_en)

Status: prior (general conventions, not calibrated on real samples) | Language: en

## Prior conventions
- Results are told in logical steps, one claim per short paragraph; claim comes first, then evidence, then figure call-out (Fig. 2a).
- First-person plural ("we") is normal; active voice; "Here we show / We find / We observe".
- Hedging is calibrated: "suggest", "indicate", "consistent with"; avoid superlatives and promotional adjectives.
- Quantities carry units, n and the test (P values, confidence intervals).
